Scan your room with your iPhone

MOZU Scan measures the room with the iPhone's LiDAR and sends it to MOZU on your computer. Nothing to install: it opens straight from this link.

Tap Open on the banner at the top of this page to start MOZU Scan. If there's no banner, open this page in Safari.

Point your iPhone's camera at this code and tap the Open card that appears:

or open on the phone

  1. Walk slowly around the room, pointing at every wall, door, window and socket.
  2. Tap Finish room, then Send to MOZU web.
  3. Type the 6-character code into MOZU on your computer.
Requires an iPhone or iPad Pro with LiDAR (iPhone 12 Pro or any later Pro model). On other phones MOZU Scan opens with a sample room so you can try the flow.

Already have a scan file? Load it on the main page instead.
